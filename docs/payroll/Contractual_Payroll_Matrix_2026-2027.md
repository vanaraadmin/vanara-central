## Payment rhythm in Central

- **1 October 2026 only:** pay the contractual October food and cash-housing advances shown below.
- **From 2 November 2026:** one payment per employee on the 2nd combines the prior month’s salary balance after employee SSO, accommodation contribution, attendance adjustments, authorised additions/deductions and contractual bonus, **plus the food and cash-housing allowance for the new month**.

- A payslip for a competence month is downloadable from the 2nd of the following month.

## Attendance, SSO and food reconciliation

- Monthly pay uses a fixed divisor of **30 days** and **8 hours per normal day**.
- Employee and employer SSO are both calculated separately from base salary only. Employer SSO is a company cost and is never deducted from the employee’s pay.
- An unpaid absence deducts the contractual daily/hourly pay. Weekly rest, paid leave (where eligible), and protected paid leave do not deduct food or salary.
- Each unique full unpaid absence can recover **50 THB** from the following month’s food advance, capped by the food advance for the source month. If the next month has no food advance, Central does not deduct automatically.
- Seasonal April-to-May reconciliation is never automatic: the contract has ended, no May advance is created, and any final adjustment needs written authorisation.
